<p class="case-section__eyebrow">Origin</p>

## Three generations of tradition, poured into one bottle.

The project stems from the union of tradition, mythology, and emotion: the creation of Xana preserves family history, passed down from grandmother to mother and from mother to daughter, while adding a touch of modernity that updates its identity without losing its authenticity.


<p class="case-section__eyebrow">Art direction</p>

## Inspired by the nymphs who guard what we love.

Inspired by the figure of Echo and the xanas, nymphs tasked with protecting loved ones, Xana is conceived as an ode to romance, inviting us to celebrate first feelings and toast to first words of love. Its identity conveys delicacy, authenticity, and an emotional connection to local tradition and culture.

<p class="case-section__eyebrow">Label Design</p>

## Water, words and echoes, wrapped around the bottle.

The label depicts the waves created by water when it hears words of love, becoming an extension of those words—and, therefore, their echo.
